Retail 2025 Web Platform

Project Overview

We developed a high-converting, fully customized e-commerce platform designed to offer a seamless shopping experience for a major retail client. The platform was built from the ground up to ensure lightning-fast page loads, intuitive navigation, and a frictionless checkout process.

Faced with the challenge of migrating hundreds of thousands of SKUs from a legacy system, our team created specialized migration scripts that mapped product data securely while preserving SEO authority.

Key Features

  • Dynamic inventory tracking with real-time supplier sync.
  • Advanced product filtering and AI-driven search capabilities.
  • Multi-currency support and automated localized tax calculation.
  • Headless commerce architecture to support omnichannel sales.

The Results

Post-launch, the client saw a 45% increase in conversion rates and a 30% reduction in cart abandonment. The modern technology stack reduced server costs by optimizing resource usage, proving that technical excellence directly drives business growth.

Technologies Used

React.js Next.js Node.js Stripe API PostgreSQL Redis

Client Needs

The client required a platform that wouldn't crash during major holiday sales and allowed marketing teams to update content without deploying code.
